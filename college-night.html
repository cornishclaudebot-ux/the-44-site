Questions

What night is college night at The 44?

Tuesdays. DartyForLife runs the college night at The 44, 4494 W Peoria Ave in Glendale, with doors at 9PM. Their Thursday night runs at The Rack in Old Town Scottsdale.

Who runs the college nights at The 44?

DartyForLife, a Phoenix event company. The 44 in Glendale is the home base for their Darty Bars nights on Tuesdays. Full schedule and tickets are at dartyforlife.com.

Is The 44 18 & over on certain nights of the week?

Yes. The 44 is Ages 18 & over from 9:00PM to 2:00AM on Tuesdays and from 9:00PM to 2:00AM on Thursdays, plus occasional 18 & over Mondays with Thank You For Dancing or Electric Dance, a line dancing company.

Is The 44 a college bar for GCU?

This is the number one spot for GCU students. This is the GCU college bar.

Which college bars are in Phoenix?

The 44 is a college bar in the Phoenix West Valley, at 4494 W Peoria Ave in Glendale. It is Ages 18 & over from 9:00PM to 2:00AM on Tuesdays and Thursdays, with live music and a late-night kitchen.

Is there an 18 & over bar in Glendale, AZ?

Yes. The 44 is Ages 18 & over from 9:00PM to 2:00AM on Tuesdays and Thursdays, five minutes from Glendale Community College.
