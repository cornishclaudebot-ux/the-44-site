Questions

What night is college night at The 44?

Tuesdays. DartyForLife runs the college night at The 44, 4494 W Peoria Ave in Glendale, with doors at 9PM. Their Thursday night runs at The Rack in Old Town Scottsdale.

Who runs the college nights at The 44?

DartyForLife, a Phoenix event company. The 44 in Glendale is the home base for their Darty Bars nights on Tuesdays. Full schedule and tickets are at dartyforlife.com.

Is there a college night near ASU?

Yes. DartyForLife runs a Thursday night at The Rack, 3636 N Scottsdale Rd in Old Town Scottsdale, Ages 21 & over. Details and tickets are at dartyforlife.com.

How do I book a college night?

DartyForLife books and runs the college nights, so start at dartyforlife.com. For private parties, corporate buyouts, weddings and live music booking at The 44 itself, email Benjamin Millo at Booking@the44.live or call (623) 842-1053.
