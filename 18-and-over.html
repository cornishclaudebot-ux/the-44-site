Age Policy · The 44 · Glendale, AZ

Is The 44 18 And Over?

The 44 is Ages 18 and over from 9:00PM to 2:00AM on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and 21 and over after 9:00PM on every other night. Ages 18 and over also applies on occasional line dancing Mondays, and on the Fridays and Saturdays when DartyForLife hosts an 18 and over event. Ticketed concerts set their own age limit, which is listed on that show's ticket page. A valid government-issued photo ID is required at the door on every night.

The 44 is a live music bar at 4494 W Peoria Ave in Glendale, Arizona, five minutes from Glendale Community College and a short drive from Grand Canyon University. The kitchen and the dining room open at 1:00PM and the age restriction starts at 9:00PM, when the night side of the room takes over.

Ticketed Concerts Set Their Own Age

This is the part that trips people up. The weekly Tuesday and Thursday nights are Ages 18 and over, but a ticketed concert at The 44 can be 21 and over even on those nights, and many touring shows are. That is why some ticket sites list The 44 as a 21 and over venue.

The age for any specific show is printed on that show's ticket page. If you are under 21 and coming out for a named act, check the ticket page first.

Bring A Real ID, Every Night

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID is required at the door on every night, including the Ages 18 and over nights. Arizona law requires The 44 to verify age before serving alcohol, so guests under 21 are marked at entry and are not served.

Common Questions

What nights is The 44 18 and over?

Tuesdays and Thursdays, every week, from 9:00PM to 2:00AM. The 44 is also Ages 18 and over on occasional Mondays when Thank You For Dancing or Electric Dance run a line dancing night, and on the occasional Friday or Saturday when DartyForLife hosts an 18 and over event at The 44.

Is The 44 21 and over?

After 9:00PM, yes, on every night that is not one of the Ages 18 and over nights. The 44 is 21 and over after 9:00PM on Wednesdays, Sundays, and on any Friday or Saturday without an 18 and over event. The kitchen and dining room open at 1:00PM and the age restriction begins at 9:00PM.

Can you get into The 44 at 18 on a Friday or Saturday?

Fridays and Saturdays are Ages 18 and over only when DartyForLife is hosting an 18 and over night at The 44. Those weekend nights are not every week. Check the age listed on that night's event page before you come out. Without an 18 and over event, Fridays and Saturdays are 21 and over after 9:00PM.

What time do the 18 and over nights start at The 44?

9:00PM. The Ages 18 and over nights at The 44 run from 9:00PM to 2:00AM on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 4494 W Peoria Ave in Glendale, Arizona.

Is there an 18 and over bar in Glendale, AZ?

Yes. The 44, at 4494 W Peoria Ave in Glendale, is Ages 18 and over from 9:00PM to 2:00AM on Tuesdays and Thursdays. It is five minutes from Glendale Community College and a short drive from Grand Canyon University.

Who runs the 18 and over nights at The 44?

DartyForLife, one of the biggest college promotion groups in the Phoenix Valley, runs the college nights at The 44 in Glendale. Their full schedule across Tempe, Scottsdale, Glendale, Avondale and Goodyear is at dartyforlife.com.
